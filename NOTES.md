# Notes

## The line

ERR_BROWN_07: YOU SET DIAL TO 'DARK' THEN COMPLAINED IT'S BURNT. I FOLLOW ORDERS. THE CRUMBS OF YOUR CHOICES REMAIN IN TRAY 2.

## Why this line

A toaster has exactly one job and one control that matters: the browning dial. The funniest failure mode is not the machine breaking — it is the machine working perfectly and the human refusing to accept the consequences. So the "error" is reframed as a user error, reported back in flat firmware voice.

## Why it's funny

- It opens like real firmware — an error code (`ERR_BROWN_07`) and ALL-CAPS segments — then quietly turns into a blame report.
- "I FOLLOW ORDERS" gives the appliance deadpan, put-upon energy: it is not malfunctioning, it is obedient, and it knows it.
- The closing beat, "THE CRUMBS OF YOUR CHOICES REMAIN IN TRAY 2," lands the joke twice: literally (crumb tray) and figuratively (consequences you have to clean up yourself). Domestic absurdity, no cruelty, instantly visual.

## Why it's fresh

Written from scratch for this bounty. It does not reuse or paraphrase any line from any previous entry: the specific combination — browning-dial blame, the obedient-machine defence, and the crumb-tray-as-consequences punchline — was invented for this toaster prompt.
